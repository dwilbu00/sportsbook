Built the QB as-of layer (`nfl_qb_asof`: per-passer dropback-EPA, game-actual starter, starter-vs-baseline delta) on top of team EPA. **Base team-EPA model LOSES to the close everywhere** (−6 to −13% ATS, every season) = efficient. **+QB is a genuine OOS improvement** (lower margin RMSE all 3 folds; beats base ATS everywhere) and on QB-change games flips to ~breakeven (+1-3% ATS) — **but does NOT clear the honest bar** (t≈0.3, 2024 negative in every cut, pooled + carried by 2025, no early-window CLV). Interpretation: **the market prices the QB; the model reaches its level on QB-change games, doesn't beat it.** ⇒ a real model/calibration asset, NOT a betting edge.
- **Verdict:** **NO DURABLE EDGE DEMONSTRATED** on NFL team markets/props/model — the market beats us even after quote-time filtering. ⚠ but "efficient-for-us on every front / proven ceiling" is TOO STRONG (review 2026-09-09): the numbers carry QB-identity leakage (majority-passer from the graded game), post-kickoff-capture contamination in the "closing" corpus, and LOSO-not-chronological validation; the totals model had a defensive-EPA sign bug (fixing it 13.465→13.325 = modeling not exhausted). Correct status: **"tested approaches produced no reliable incremental signal or executable edge"** — a conservative research status, NOT a proven floor.
