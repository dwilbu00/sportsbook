The bottom line after a long edge hunt on MLB: **efficient markets have no durable mean edge for us, and sharp-staleness is dead.** Two real edges survived — the DK-internal **coherence run-line** (the keystone) and the **earned_runs × high-CV variance gate** (cv_floor, LIVE). Everything else (props/team-ML/F5 vs Pinnacle, team mean-edge, retrains, competitor "edge") tested NULL, well-powered. The strategic reframe stands: edge is market STRUCTURE / variance / self-contradiction, NOT out-predicting the book.

Execution note (correction applied throughout): Doug bets **DraftKings AND FanDuel** (both executable). Pinnacle and other books are **analysis-only, never bet**. Data is **warehouse-only, 2024-2026** (2023 fully purged; local-JSON stores dead) — read via `db_store` / the `r2_*` stack / `--source warehouse`.

## ★ VALIDATED EDGE #1 — the DK-internal coherence run-line (THE keystone)
Bet DK's posted run-line where it deviates from what DK's OWN moneyline + total imply (run-distribution translation via `coherence.py` on `mlb_starters._run_pmf`), after a historical Poisson-shape offset. Needs NO sharper reference — it exploits DK contradicting ITSELF, which is why it's the one survivor after every external-sharp thesis failed.
- **Result (LOSO, leak-free):** pooled +3.79-3.86% ROI, hit 61.3%, n≈1,332, **t≈1.66**, positive all 3 seasons (2024 +4.1% / 2025 +6.5% / 2026 +0.93%), both home/away. Robust to `--ev-floor 0.05` (+5.25%) and `--haircut 0.04`; never clears t=2 (3 seasons ≈ the data ceiling). ⚠ RAW model had a systematic +0.0263 (t=24.85) Poisson-shape bias (independent Poisson overstates run-line-favorite cover; real MLB has more one-run games) — FIX = coherent-on-average calibration, default **leave-one-season-out**. Dispersion is the WRONG knob (widens margins = worse).
- **★ SHARPENED (commit bde604a) to the underdog +1.5 side only, at moderate favorites [0.60,0.70) ML-implied**, where the edge concentrates (~+11% backtest; flat <60%, negative on heavy favs ≥70%). `DEFAULT_FAV_MIN/MAX` in `coherence_flags` (tunable; 0/1 disables). Band is data-selected on 2024-26 → a FORWARD-TRACK hypothesis, not a proven +11%.
- **Corroborated independently** by `scenario_backtest.py` (`dog_runline`): underdog +1.5 with a clear ML favorite = pooled +4.20%/t=2.40, all 3 seasons; the 65-70% band = +20% ROI replicating +20.3/+18.3/+22.4% (2024/25/26), a two-bin plateau (not a knife-edge), STRONGEST in 2026 (near-close) which kills the stale-early-line explanation. The `coherence_backtest` convergence check (367e0ad) confirmed coherence flags are **100% dog+1.5, ZERO favorite -1.5** — coherence literally IS a "bet the dog +1.5" strategy, and its pooling of all favorite strengths is what diluted it to t=1.66. Two independent methods on the same inefficiency = the strongest corroboration we've had.
- **SHIPPED in-app** (commit 0a2725f on adapters a198b12): own "🎯 Coherence Run-Line" section, competes in the top-N auto-pick, auto-logged to the forward log as distinct bet_type `runline_coherence` regardless of whether Doug places it — **the in-app forward log IS the confirmation instrument**. Offset fit once/slate (`coherence_flags.compute_offset`, 3-season SQL read, cached; skips if it can't fit). Standalone `coherence_flags.py --live` exists for terminal use. 3 audit bugs fixed (15818a1: DH-safe game_pk stamping, same-team double-select in auto-picker, 0.0-offset empty-fit guard). MLB-only. **Bet small until the forward record grows.**

## ★ VALIDATED EDGE #2 — earned_runs × high-CV (cv_floor, the first variance edge)
Bet `pitcher_earned_runs` only on the most volatile starters (recency-weighted CV ≥ 1.3) — the market underprices variance on fat-tailed, blow-up-prone starters. Pre-registered ([[market-upset-study]]), so it lifts above data-mining.
- **Decisive OOS test PASSED:** staged a 2024-trained candidate (method C→E), graded 2025+2026 × close+early = **4/4 clean-OOS cells positive**, monotone volatile-only stratification held all 4: close pooled 352 bets +9.47% t≈1.9; **early pooled 410 bets +11.20% t≈2.5** (early = the price Doug bets, clears 2σ). Survived on the WORSE-calibrated E model → the edge is market MISPRICING of variance, not our accuracy. SPECIFIC to earned_runs (the same CV≥1.3 cut is NEGATIVE on every batter prop — not a generic artifact). EV-floor invariant (CV≥1.3 bets are already high-EV; pooled EV≥4% = +11.1%/409).
- **LIVE:** `earned_runs` serves **method E (NegBin) + the cv_floor gate** — NOT method A/C, and deliberately NOT Platt-calibrated (a global Platt is dominated by low-CV starters and would over-shrink the already-calibrated high-CV subset; earned_runs is only ever bet via the CV gate). Gate = `props._recency_weighted_cv` + `value_gate.cv_floor{"pitcher_earned_runs":1.3}` (commit 9d63e68 built, 245aa0b LIVE), CV computable live at zero I/O. ⚠ Required a `load_calibration` candidate-serving fix (c6603a9 + a6fd387) — props were team-market-only; first grade attempt read LIVE, caught by sanity check.
- Open: forward-monitor (structure edges decay) + confirm DK/FD fillability on volatile SPs at size; do NOT anchor on the partial-season Kelly line.
- **Owner hypothesis TESTED → REFUTED + INVERTED (2026-08-28, `scenario_backtest --scenario team_variance`).** Bucketing team ROI by the starter's ER-CV (the cv_floor signal, half_life=5): high SP volatility makes team bets WORSE, monotonically, replicating all 3 seasons — total OVER −10/−17/−27% across CV <1.0 / 1.0-1.3 / ≥1.3; dog ML +2.9/+0.1/−17.9%; dog +1.5 +6.5/−2.2/−10.7%. WHY: ER-CV=σ/μ and a LOW mean inflates CV, so high-CV flags run-SUPPRESSING starters (lower-scoring games) → unders/favorites, NOT overs/dogs. Variance-as-underpricing does NOT carry to team markets ⇒ Monte Carlo shape-sim = no-go on this basis. BYPRODUCT (forward-track, don't backtest-ship — overfit risk): the dog+1.5 edge concentrates in LOW-CV (stable) favorite games (+6.5% t=3.18) and dies on high-CV favorites → a candidate stable-favorite-SP refinement to the coherence flag. Weather/umpire-as-instability is a separate, still-untested form.

## ✗ EXHAUSTED / DEAD (do not re-litigate)
- **Sharp-staleness (R2) — REFUTED across all 3 executable markets, well-powered.** DK is an EFFICIENT book for us: props (Brier tie DK 0.2443 vs Pin 0.2444, 65k legs), team ML (tie 0.2455 = 0.2455, 2,195 events), F5 ML (tie 0.2412 vs 0.2413, 2,679, via `f5_backtest.py` commit fcaa8df). Pinnacle is NOT sharper than DK on any of them. The sharpness test became the GATE that killed R2 before we built a decomposition on a false premise (Doug's repeated "is Pinnacle even sharper?" check). ⇒ Odds-shopping DK-vs-Pinnacle only reduces vig, doesn't create +EV; DK-vs-FanDuel line-shopping (both executable) is a real ~1-4% execution multiplier on an edge you ALREADY have, not a standalone edge.
- **The R2 cross-line projector is a −8% ARTIFACT.** DK hits-0.5 priced off Pinnacle TB-1.5 via Poisson projection = `batter_hits projected n=10,216 ROI −8.41% t=−12.44`, replicating all 3 seasons. The projection overstates the fair → phantom +EV overs that lose. Answers Doug's TB-1.5→hits-0.5 question: NO. Don't bet or serve projected legs.
- **Props over-bias = asymmetric vig, NOT bettable.** Side-split (commit 0a68947) found a strong replicating DK recreational OVER-bias (TB over −11.4%, ER over −13.6% / ER under ≈breakeven, hits over −9.5%; pitcher_outs REVERSED = internal-consistency proof it's real). BUT the sharpness tie means DK's devigged PROBS are accurate → the bias is just more juice on overs, not a prediction error. ER-under is less-vigged, not +EV. Flat-betting the favored side is not profitable after vig.
- **Team markets — NO durable mean edge.** Fresh full re-calibration (Gaps A/B/C/E tooling) proved calibration is HONEST (Arm C beats market Brier OOS) but Brier-optimal ≠ bettable, and no edge replicates: moneyline small-edge is the only thing that ever survived exact replication (≤1-2% gate, >2% tail loses, ~2σ, fragile, carried by partial-2026) but the clean-data per-season verdict (2023 +7.9% / 2024 −9.9% / 2025 −7.4%) = variance, not edge. Spreads = a mirage (needed two stacked tailwinds; sign-flips across holdouts). Totals = abstain (suppressed). **Stop manufacturing a team edge.** Live config for reference: `prob_shrink`={spreads 0.6, totals 0.15, moneyline 0.35}, `market_blend`=None, `value_gate.suppress`=['pitcher_outs','totals'], `DEFAULT_PYTHAG_WEIGHT`=0.50.
- **Retrain 2024-2025 (exclude-2023), validate 2026 = clean NULL, props AND teams.** Narrowing to the recent clean regime still doesn't hold in the following season; the market re-prices faster than any static annual refit. Only the within-season online Platt loop helps (Brier, not ROI). Sample-size abstention REFUTED (losses live in the high-n bulk, not the low-n tail).
- **Cross-market coherence probes (props↔team) = confounded/exhausted.** Props snapshots are tight closes (~99% ≤15m); team snapshots are coarse whole-slate captures (hours pre-commence, close-only) → props↔team "incoherence" is a timing artifact, uncalibratable. The team-triad ML/RL/total (all one snapshot = clean) was the one clean cross-market test → the run-line edge. Within-props/within-team = same-desk (low prior). Multibook/coherence program COMPLETE.
- **Competitor `baseball-predictions` "beats-market Brier" = an ARTIFACT** (target leakage, fabricated flat -110, no forward/CLV record). Measured honestly their clean Brier ≈ our null. Closes the "are we behind" question; reinforces no-durable-edge. Real takeaway = a few soft-market feature IDEAS only.
- **Free-screen feature triage COMPLETE** (do not re-flag): A1 lineup-runs = DEAD (doesn't beat recency), A3 bullpen-fatigue = marginal/not-shipped, A9 alt pitcher sets = no win, B3 spread-dispersion = keep d=0. NegBin-E overdispersion (#5) rejected for live (fattening tails strips the confident calls that carried ROI). Uncertainty-Kelly-via-n_eff DROPPED for teams (recency-window n_eff is the wrong uncertainty source); sizing verdict for the thin team edge = FLAT (Kelly amplifies drawdown savagely). ## Strategic inflection (where the hunt landed)
Sharp-staleness AND cross-market coherence probes are both exhausted on efficient MLB markets. The two live edges (coherence run-line marginal; cv_floor variance) are shipped. Genuinely-different next directions Doug is steering (NOT more efficient-MLB probes): (a) deepen coherence — a Monte Carlo sim that prices all markets off one distribution and flags every DK self-inconsistency at once (a DIFFERENT edge type: correlation/parlay, not prediction); (b) accept the null + forward-track the run-line at small stakes; (c) soft/niche markets or other sports (NBA/NFL); (d) consolidate + operate.
