# SUBSYSTEM
MLB team totals / game totals / pace / implied runs — the raw run-environment material a per-game GameContext would draw on (odds_client totals ingest, mlb_starters expected-runs analytic run model, analysis team-market consumers, park_factors, team offensive-rate feeds).

## FUNCTIONS
- **odds_client.parse_game_odds** (deploy/odds_client.py:568-632)
  Parses ONE game's odds into {game_id, home_team, away_team, commence_time, moneyline, spreads, totals}. The O/U game total is ingested here: result['totals']['Over'|'Under'] = [{book, line(point), price}]. This is the ONLY place a betting game total (O/U) enters the system. It is parsed from the team-market odds payload, NOT the props payload.
- **analysis.analyze_totals_value** (deploy/analysis.py:341-532)
  The sole consumer of the ingested O/U total. Picks the modal consensus_line, builds projected_total purely from ESPN recent-game runs (home/away avg_scored+avg_allowed)/2 plus a starter/bullpen run-suppression shift, then scores P(over) via a Normal(projected_total, total_std). The market line is used ONLY as the strike point and (optionally, blend_w<1) as a de-vigged blend target — it is NOT converted into an implied team run total, and nothing here flows to player props.
- **mlb_starters.build_matchup_features** (deploy/mlb_starters.py:867-1013)
  THE current per-game 'matchup_features' object 3.1 wants to promote. Returns {home:{starter,opp_offense_vs_hand,bullpen}, away:{...}, starter_edge (tanh, [-1,1]), expected_runs:{complete, home_offense_factor, away_offense_factor, home_staff_suppression, away_staff_suppression}}. The expected_runs block is the leakage-safe league-relative (xwOBA) raw material for per-team run means.
- **mlb_starters.get_expected_runs_team_factors** (deploy/mlb_starters.py:194-312)
  Leakage-SAFE team offensive/pitching rate feed. Returns {league_xwoba, league_bullpen_xwoba, offense_vs_hand:{L:{ABBR:xwoba}, R:{...}}, bullpen_xwoba:{ABBR:xwoba}} from Baseball Savant statcast_search CSV, restricted to games strictly before as_of (cutoff = as_of - 1 day via game_date_lt). Team keys normalized to StatsAPI abbrs. Cached 24h.
- **mlb_starters.expected_runs_from_factors** (deploy/mlb_starters.py:350-375)
  Analytic per-team expected-runs closed form: expected = base_runs * offense_factor**offense_weight / staff_suppression**pitching_weight, clamped [0.5,12.0]. Pure, stdlib. This is the per-team run-mean generator a shared GameContext would call twice (home offense vs away staff, away offense vs home staff).
- **mlb_starters.poisson_margin_probability** (deploy/mlb_starters.py:393-424)
  Analytic independent-Poisson JOINT over the (home_score, away_score) grid (max_runs=30): builds two Poisson score vectors from home_runs/away_runs and sums the joint where home_score+home_spread>away_score. This is the existing analytic-moment joint precedent — a GameContext total distribution is the convolution of these same two Poisson vectors.
- **mlb_starters.negative_binomial_margin_probability** (deploy/mlb_starters.py:427-472)
  Over-dispersed variant of the joint (variance = mean + dispersion*mean^2); dispersion=0 delegates to poisson_margin_probability. Same score-grid summation. Relevant if the GameContext total needs heavier baseball tails than Poisson.
- **analysis._mlb_expected_runs_projection** (deploy/analysis.py:136-192)
  Turns matchup_features.expected_runs + the calibration challenger config into {home_runs, away_runs, margin, spread_share, margin_share}. Reads home_base_runs/away_base_runs/offense_weight/pitching_weight from calibration JSON's expected_runs_challenger.final_2025_validation.model. Gated by expected_runs_challenger.enabled AND live_markets.spreads. This is the closest thing to a live per-game run-total producer; it currently emits run MEANS but no exposed distribution object and is spread-only.
- **mlb_starters.get_team_offense_splits** (deploy/mlb_starters.py:771-801)
  Team OPS + k_pct split by opposing pitcher hand ({vL:{ops,k_pct}, vR:{...}}) from MLB StatsAPI season statSplits. Season-to-date, NOT date-sliced (leaks in backtest). Feeds the starter_edge _off_factor and the pitcher-prop matchup multiplier.
- **mlb_starters.get_team_bullpen_quality** (deploy/mlb_starters.py:804-831)
  Team bullpen {rp_era, bullpen_suppression = LEAGUE_AVG.era/rp_era clamped} from StatsAPI season pitching splits (rp). Season-to-date, NOT date-sliced. Feeds staff_factor and the totals starter/bullpen shift.
- **espn_client.compute_recent_form + recent_games total_score** (deploy/espn_client.py:185-202 (scores), 287-352 (form))
  The runs/game source for team markets: each schedule game carries home_score, away_score, total_score=home+away; compute_recent_form + analyze_totals_value._weighted_team_scoring produce recency/venue-weighted avg_scored/avg_allowed. As-of-safe live (only completed games); backtest slices via prior_games.
- **stats.hits_at_least / negbin_at_least / _norm_cdf** (deplof/stats.py:152-221, 14-16)
  Pure-stdlib analytic count survivals: hits_at_least(k,n,p) binomial, negbin_at_least(k,mean,dispersion) over-dispersed count, _norm_cdf Gaussian. These are the moment-matching primitives a GameContext (and its per-batter reconciliation to team hits) must reuse instead of Monte-Carlo.
- **props.analyze_player_props_value** (deploy/props.py:862-1553+)
  Player-prop projector. Batter base_proj = recency/shrinkage-weighted mean hits (props.py:1122-1127), scaled by combined_mult (output_def*matchup*lineup*park*weather*rest, props.py:1251-1254). Computes per-batter expected_ab/expected_exposure (player_context, props.py:1183-1195). Projects EACH batter INDEPENDENTLY — no team aggregation, no shared run/total context, no O/U input. Signature ends with matchup_features=None, weather=None (props.py:865-866): the natural place to add a game_context= param.
- **props._mlb_prop_matchup_mult** (deploy/props.py:93-162)
  Bounded [0.7,1.4] per-prop multiplier from matchup_features: batter_hits scales by opposing starter xBA via log5 (starter_share*starter_rate + bullpen_rate); pitcher props by opposing lineup OPS/k_pct vs hand. Consumes matchup_features but produces only a scalar mult, not a joint.
- **espn_client.get_team_pace_factor** (deploy/espn_client.py:210-256)
  ESPN paceFactor (possessions/game) — a BASKETBALL concept. Cached in backtest.cached_pace_factor and used only in the NBA player-prop sweep (backtest.py:2207-2240, 2599-2603). There is NO MLB pace/run-environment analog; 'pace' in this repo is NBA-only and irrelevant to a GameContext run model.

## DATA_FLOW
INGEST: Live app fans out per event (app.py ~2450-2550) — odds futures -> parse_game_odds (totals here), prop-odds futures -> parse_player_props (NO totals), team schedules -> ESPN recent_games (runs/game), weather futures -> event_weather[eid], and for MLB build_matchup_features(home,away,ET-date,season) at app.py:2619. TWO SEPARATE downstream branches: (1) team markets — analyze_moneyline/spreads/totals_value(game_odds, home_stats, away_stats, matchup_features) at app.py:2708-2712; (2) player props — analyze_player_props_value(prop_data, player_histories, matchup_features, weather=event_weather[eid]) at app.py:2740-2748. The two branches never share a run distribution.\n\nSHAPES: game_odds.totals = {'Over':[{book,line,price}], 'Under':[...]}. matchup_features = {'home':{'starter':{era,xera,xwoba,xba,run_suppression,avg_ip,k_pct,bf,throws}, 'opp_offense_vs_hand':{ops,k_pct}, 'bullpen':{rp_era,bullpen_suppression}}, 'away':{...}, 'starter_edge':float, 'expected_runs':{'complete':bool,'home_offense_factor','away_offense_factor','home_staff_suppression','away_staff_suppression'}}. expected_runs factors are ~1.0-centered league-relative multipliers.\n\nTRANSFORM (run model, spread-only today): analysis._mlb_expected_runs_projection reads expected_runs + calibration challenger model {offense_weight=1.25, pitching_weight=0.75, home_base_runs=4.423, away_base_runs=4.429} -> expected_runs_from_factors -> {home_runs, away_runs, margin} -> poisson_margin_probability(home_runs,away_runs,spread) -> expected_home_cover, blended into the spread cover by ensemble_challenger_share.home_minus_1_5=0.7 / margin=0.9 (analysis.py:571-710). score_distribution='independent_poisson' in the config.\n\nTRANSFORM (totals market): analyze_totals_value builds projected_total from recent-game runs + starter/bullpen shift and scores it against the modal O/U line via Normal; the O/U total is a strike/blend input only.\n\nTRANSFORM (batter prop): per player, base_proj (weighted mean hits) x combined_mult -> avg_stat; over-rate via empirical / method B (Gaussian residual) / D (binomial hits_at_least on per-AB p and expected_ab n) / E (negbin_at_least). expected_ab/expected_exposure computed per batter but never summed across a lineup.\n\nEXIT: candidate dicts to app.py for display; predictions logged for later Platt recal.

## GAMECONTEXT_RELEVANCE
WHAT EXISTS (usable raw material): (1) An analytic per-game two-team run model already lives here — expected_runs_from_factors gives per-team run MEANS and poisson_margin_probability/negative_binomial_margin_probability already form the independent-Poisson JOINT over the score grid; score_distribution is explicitly 'independent_poisson'. A total distribution is just the convolution of the two per-team Poisson vectors that poisson_margin_probability already builds internally. (2) Leakage-safe team offense/pitching rates by hand exist via get_expected_runs_team_factors (xwOBA, bullpen xwOBA, as-of cutoff). (3) The O/U market total IS ingested (parse_game_odds) and is the market's own total-distribution mean. (4) Per-batter expected_ab / expected_exposure is already computed (props.py:1183-1195) — the exposure counts needed to make sum-of-batter-hits reconcile to a team hit total. (5) All stdlib analytic primitives for count sums exist (stats.hits_at_least, negbin_at_least, _norm_cdf).\n\nWHAT IS MISSING for a shared per-game GameContext: (a) No object holds a shared run/pace/total distribution — expected_runs is recomputed inline in analysis._mlb_expected_runs_projection and thrown away; nothing exposes {home_runs, away_runs, total_dist}. (b) The run model is gated spread-ONLY (expected_runs_challenger.live_markets: spreads=true, totals=false, moneyline=false) so no game-total distribution is emitted anywhere today. (c) The O/U market total never reaches props: parse_player_props DROPS totals and analyze_player_props_value has no game_odds/total parameter — the market total is genuinely unavailable at prop-projection time without new wiring. (d) Batter props are projected fully independently; there is NO cross-batter aggregation and NO reconciliation of sum(batter hits) -> team hits -> team runs -> game total. (e) home_base_runs/away_base_runs are STATIC fitted league constants (4.423/4.429); per-game variation enters only through offense_factor/staff_suppression — a GameContext would want to also fold in the O/U-implied total and park/weather. (f) No MLB pace concept exists (paceFactor is NBA-only).

## INTEGRATION_SEAMS
- PRODUCE (build once per game): app.py:2619 `matchup_features = mlb_starters.build_matchup_features(home, away, game_date, int(game_date[:4]))` is the exact spot — a GameContext should be built immediately after, mirroring the per-event weather pattern at app.py:2543-2550 (`event_weather[eid] = fut.result()`), and cached per eid the same way to avoid per-Streamlit-rerun recompute.
- PRODUCE (run means already available): analysis._mlb_expected_runs_projection(sport_key, matchup_features) -> {home_runs, away_runs, margin, spread_share, margin_share} (deploy/analysis.py:136-192); the underlying generator is mlb_starters.expected_runs_from_factors(base_runs, offense_factor, staff_suppression, offense_weight=1.0, pitching_weight=1.0) (deploy/mlb_starters.py:350-375). A GameContext would call this to get home_runs/away_runs, then reuse the Poisson score-vector construction from mlb_starters.poisson_margin_probability (deploy/mlb_starters.py:393-424) to expose a total distribution.
- INGEST market total into the context: odds_client.parse_game_odds -> game_odds['totals'] (deploy/odds_client.py:619-630) currently only reaches the team-market branch (app.py:2650-2712). To feed props, either thread game_odds into the props branch or extend parse_player_props (deploy/odds_client.py:944-960, which today returns only game_id/home/away/commence/sport_key/props) to carry the consensus O/U line.
- CONSUME in props: props.analyze_player_props_value(prop_data, player_histories, ... matchup_features=None, weather=None) (signature deploy/props.py:862-866) needs a new `game_context=` param, threaded from the call site app.py:2740-2748. The per-batter base rate to reconcile is set at props.py:1122-1127 (base_proj) and props.py:1251-1254 (combined_mult -> avg_stat); the per-batter exposure n is player_context expected_exposure/expected_ab at props.py:1183-1195.
- CONSUME analytically (no Monte-Carlo): the binomial per-batter -> team-hits sum and any total reconciliation must use stats.hits_at_least(k,n,p) / stats.negbin_at_least(k,mean,dispersion) / stats._norm_cdf (deploy/stats.py:152-221, 14-16), the same closed forms method D/E already use at props.py:1276-1318.
- CONFIG gate: analysis._EXPECTED_RUNS_CACHE loads calibration['expected_runs_challenger'] via calibration_loader.load_expected_runs_challenger (deploy/calibration_loader.py:182-186); the params live in calibration/baseball_mlb.json:7868-7986 (enabled, live_markets, final_2025_validation.model {offense_weight,pitching_weight,home_base_runs,away_base_runs}, ensemble_challenger_share). A GameContext for props needs its own enable flag / live_markets extension here rather than silently reusing the spread-only gate.

## RISKS
- LEAKAGE (asymmetric): only get_expected_runs_team_factors is as-of-safe (cutoff=as_of-1d). build_matchup_features ALSO calls get_team_offense_splits, get_team_bullpen_quality, get_pitcher_quality which are SEASON-TO-DATE (not date-sliced) and leak in backtest (_mlb_matchup_features at backtest.py:997-1013 passes only season, no slice). A GameContext must source its run means from the expected_runs (xwOBA) path — NOT the OPS/ERA starter_edge path — to stay leakage-safe when re-validated.
- PER-RERUN COST / CACHING: build_matchup_features makes many StatsAPI + Savant HTTP calls; it is rebuilt on every 'Analyze' pass (no Streamlit memoization; only file-level _read_cache/_write_cache with CACHE_MAX_AGE + a 24h Savant cache). A GameContext must be built once per game and cached per eid (like event_weather), and it must NOT Monte-Carlo per rerun — the design constraint is analytic moment-matching only.
- ANALYTIC BUDGET / stdlib-only: no numpy/scipy. poisson_margin_probability is O(max_runs^2)=~900 ops per game (fine), but a per-batter binomial convolution to a team-hits distribution over 9 hitters must stay bounded/closed-form (moment-match to a Normal/NegBin via stats primitives) rather than exact enumeration to avoid blowup.
- DOUBLE-COUNTING: combined_mult already applies park (_park_factor_mult) and weather (_weather_factor_mult) and the log5 starter matchup to each batter's base_proj (props.py:1251-1254). If a GameContext run/total also embeds park/weather/starter, reconciling batter hits to that total will double-count those factors unless one side is neutralized.
- MARKET-TOTAL AVAILABILITY: the O/U total only exists in game_odds when totals were fetched (market_keys includes 'totals') AND eid in odds_results; the props branch runs independently and can fire when the team-market branch didn't. Any GameContext depending on the market total must fail open when the total is absent (mirror the fail-open discipline of weather/lineup gating).
- COUPLING TO STATIC BASELINES: home_base_runs/away_base_runs are fixed fitted constants (4.423/4.429); per-game signal is only offense_factor/staff_suppression. A GameContext that ignores the O/U-implied total will systematically miss game-specific run environment (blowout/pitchers-duel) that the market prices in.
- GATE REUSE: expected_runs_challenger.live_markets currently disables totals and moneyline; reusing analysis._mlb_expected_runs_projection for props would inherit its spread-only gate and its ensemble_challenger_share semantics. A props GameContext needs its own config/enable path so it can't be silently turned off (or on) by the spread challenger's flags.
