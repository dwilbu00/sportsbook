## approach_name
Design B — roadmap-faithful full-joint bottom-up GameContext with a three-tier validator stack

## one_line_thesis
Promote the discarded per-game run joint (expected_runs xwOBA means -> NegBin team-run PMFs) into a shared, cached, leakage-safe GameContext; reconcile the confirmed-lineup batter-hit sum to the context-derived team-hit mean by analytic moment-matching, add a shared game-offense latent for variance+correlation, and validate the three model layers with three matched gates: the existing real-line Brier gate for the mean shift, a NEW distributional method "G" for the full marginal, and a NEW same-game parlay-CLV harness for the correlation the single-prop Brier is blind to.

## scope_summary
BUILT: (1) a new game_context.py holding a GameContext(home,away,date) with the two team run means (expected_runs xwOBA path ONLY, leakage-safe), each team's 0..30 NegBin run PMF (reusing mlb_starters' PMF recurrence, factored public), the convolved game-total distribution, a league hits-per-run constant rho mapping run-mean->team-hit-mean, the confirmed ordered lineup with per-slot AB share, and per-batter phase-1 (p_AB, expected AB). (2) Bottom-up reconciliation: sum-of-batter-hits <-> team-hit-mean (=rho*mu_R) <-> team-run-mean <-> game total, producing a per-batter scalar reconcile mult gamma and a per-batter allocation. (3) A shared game-offense latent lambda giving analytic variance inflation + batter-to-batter correlation (small discretized lambda grid, NO Monte-Carlo). (4) Three validators: gamma rides the EXISTING real-line Brier gate as a scalar; a NEW distributional method "G" (full lambda-marginalized binomial) wired into _score_abc_real+candidate_methods; a NEW game-oriented parlay-CLV harness for correlation. NOT BUILT/OUT OF SCOPE: replacing the team totals/spread pricing engines themselves (GameContext feeds them but analyze_totals_value stays until separately gated); any market-line blend in the offline validation (leakage); numpy/scipy anything; the synthetic-sweep leg for this feature (deliberately bypassed, matchup_mult precedent).

## gamecontext_object
Shape (plain dict/dataclass, stdlib): GameContext = {home_team, away_team, game_date, as_of (=game_date-1d), complete:bool, mu_R:{home,away} (expected runs MEAN per team), run_pmf:{home:[p0..p30], away:[...]}, total_dist:{mean,var,pmf}, rho (league team-hits-per-run constant, ~1.45, a committed leakage-safe scalar not a per-game fetch), team_hit_mean:{home,away}=rho*mu_R, lineup:{home:[{player_id,slot,ab_share}], away:[...]}, batters:{player_id->{side,p_AB,expected_ab,mu_i}}, lambda_grid:[(weight,lambda)] shared game-offense latent, tau2 (latent variance = run-PMF overdispersion/mu_R^2)}. BUILT WHERE: new game_context.build_game_context(home,away,date,season) — mu_R sourced ONLY from mlb_starters.get_expected_runs_team_factors(season,as_of)+expected_runs_from_factors (the sole as-of-safe path; NEVER get_team_offense_splits/bullpen/pitcher_quality which are season-to-date and LEAK); run_pmf from a newly factored public mlb_starters.build_team_run_pmf(mean,dispersion,max_runs) (extracted verbatim from the inner probabilities() of negative_binomial_margin_probability so the joint that is currently discarded after the spread scalar is now retained); lineup from get_confirmed_lineup/lineup_player_context; per-batter p_AB/expected_ab reuse the _distributional_over_rate per-AB machinery. CACHED WHERE: module-level game_context._CTX_CACHE keyed (home,away,date) mirroring game_results._SCORE_CACHE:37 and the lineups 5-min cache; completeness guard complete=(both mu_R present AND >=1 lineup side confirmed) and a short TTL so a stale probable/lineup cannot pin a mispriced game; runtime builds it once per Analyze click alongside matchup_features (app.py:2619). LEAKAGE HANDLING: mu_R as-of (as_of=game_date-1d, xwOBA factors); lineup/probable are pre-game facts (cannot leak); rho is a fixed committed constant. TTL/COMPLETENESS: incomplete -> GameContext returned with complete=False and every consumer FAILS OPEN to today's per-player independent pricing (no reconciliation, no method G p_dist emitted for that game).

## math
PIPELINE. Run means: mu_R^team = expected_runs_from_factors(base, offense_factor, opp_staff_suppression, w_off, w_pit) (mlb_starters:350), leakage-safe xwOBA inputs. Team-run dist: run_pmf^team = build_team_run_pmf(mu_R, phi, 30) using the NegBin recurrence P(s)=P(s-1)*(s-1+size)/s*failure_p, size=1/phi (the factored inner of negative_binomial_margin_probability:451; phi = the committed run dispersion). Game total: total_pmf = discrete convolution of the two team PMFs (reuse the same double-loop as poisson_margin_probability:419 but KEEP the full joint instead of collapsing to a spread scalar); total mean/var by moments. Team-runs->team-hits: first-moment map mu_H^team = rho * mu_R^team (rho committed league E[H]/E[R]); team-hit variance Var_H = rho^2*Var(run_pmf) (moment-matched, over-dispersed vs Poisson). Per-batter phase 1: for batter i, p_AB,i and n_i=expected_ab,i from his own as-of prior games (stats._weighted_mean of hits/AB and AB/game; xBA blend via _dist_p_over level exactly as runtime); binomial moments mu_i=n_i*p_AB,i, sigma2_i=n_i*p_AB,i*(1-p_AB,i) (stats.hits_at_least is the survival used at pricing). Allocation + reconciliation: raw lineup sum S=sum_i mu_i (RAW base rate*exposure, NOT combined_mult, to avoid re-encoding matchup/slot); reconcile scalar gamma^team = clamp(mu_H^team / S, [0.7,1.4]) — the analytic moment-match of the lineup first moment to the context team-hit mean; per-batter reconciled mean mu_i' = mu_i*gamma (equivalently p_AB,i' = clamp(p_AB,i*gamma)); slot allocation preserved because mu_i already carries n_i (slot AB share). Variance/correlation: shared game-offense latent lambda ~ mean 1, var tau2 (tau2 = Var(run_pmf)/mu_R^2 minus the within-binomial share, floored at 0), discretized to a small K-point grid {(w_g,lambda_g)} by Gauss-Hermite-style moment matching (stats._norm_ppf to place nodes; NO MC). Marginal per batter: P_i(over L) = sum_g w_g * hits_at_least(int(L)+1, n_i, clamp(p_AB,i'*lambda_g)) — this inflates each batter's tail (variance) AND, because lambda is SHARED, induces the positive same-game batter correlation used only by parlays. Reconciliation identity check: sum_i mu_i' == mu_H^team == rho*mu_R^team, and sum of both teams' mu_R feeds the total_dist mean that REPLACES analyze_totals_value.projected_total (analysis.py:400) and the run means that REPLACE the spread-only _mlb_expected_runs_projection (analysis.py:136) — the three previously-unreconciled run estimates now share one source. Stdlib primitives reused: mlb_starters.build_team_run_pmf(new, factored), stats.hits_at_least:152, stats.negbin_at_least:183, stats._weighted_mean/_weighted_std, stats._norm_cdf/_norm_ppf, props._dist_p_over:424.

## gate_story
THREE LAYERS, THREE MATCHED VALIDATORS. (1) MEAN SHIFT (gate-visible today): the per-batter scalar feat_mult is gamma^team (the reconcile mult for the batter's side). It reaches the per-player offline obs via a NEW per-game augmentation seam INSIDE join_book_lines_to_actuals (book_line_calibration:339): after the existing enrichment, a pre-pass groups the enriched batter_hits obs into games by (game_date, home_team, away_team) — all four fields ALREADY on each row (verified: rows carry home_team/away_team/game_date/player_mlb_id) — determines each batter's side from test_game.is_home, builds a leakage-safe GameContext (mu_R from as-of expected_runs, per-batter mu_i from each obs's OWN prior_games), computes gamma^team, and attaches obs['game_ctx_mult']=gamma. project_and_empirical then rides the EXISTING scalar seam (:598-606): projected*=gamma; line_eff=line/gamma — so gamma moves methods A/B/C/E and clears (or fails) select_method_at_real_lines:1011 + _variant_confirms unchanged. This is the FIRST gate verdict. (2) FULL MARGINAL (mean+variance): a NEW distributional method "G" wired into _score_abc_real (:865) exactly like method D — book_line_calibration.project_game_joint(obs, game_ctx) returns a split-independent per-row p_dist = the lambda-marginalized sum_g w_g*hits_at_least(...); add has_g=all(r.get('p_dist_g') is not None), scores['G']=_brier(pG,out), probs['G']=pG; add 'G' to candidate_methods and select_method_at_real_lines's confirm loop. Because a variance change DOES move P(over) at lines OFF the mean, single-prop real-line Brier is PARTIALLY sensitive to G's variance term (only a mean-preserving change exactly AT the line is invisible). (3) CORRELATION (Brier-invisible entirely): validated OUTSIDE the gate by a NEW game-oriented parlay-CLV harness (backtest_props.py precedent, the same place _mlb_prop_matchup_mult is validated off-gate): build same-game 2-3-leg batter_hits parlays, price under (a) independence vs (b) the shared-lambda joint (reads each candidate's _values/_weights + the shared lambda grid), score parlay log-loss/Brier against realized joint outcomes and CLV against DK closes. This is the ONLY correlation-honest validator.

## parity_plan
Parity becomes TWO-WAY not three-way, by design. A shared game_context.py (build_game_context + reconcile_batter + project_game_joint p_dist) is imported by BOTH (a) runtime props.py combined_mult (new game_ctx_mult factor, :1251) and (b) offline book_line_calibration (the join-time augmentation + method G p_dist), so those two cannot drift — the same invariant that keeps rest/xBA aligned via prop_features.projection_multiplier. The THIRD leg — the synthetic sweep backtest.run_player_props_backtest (:2652) — is DELIBERATELY BYPASSED for this feature because the sweep loop is per-player with SYNTHETIC lines and carries no opposing-team/lineup/run-total structure (backtest.py:2329); it cannot construct a GameContext, so it cannot compute gamma or the lambda joint. This is the exact matchup_mult precedent (runtime-only, validated off the sweep in backtest_props.py) called out in the mandate. Consequence made explicit: the sweep-axis auto-adoption (_best_per_prop) CANNOT size this feature; only the method-axis real-line gate (select_method_at_real_lines: gamma via A-E, and method G) can adopt it. A parity unit test asserts runtime game_ctx_mult == offline game_ctx_mult on a fixed fixture game; a second asserts project_game_joint p_dist == the runtime lambda-marginalized P(over) for the same obs.

## double_counting_mitigation
combined_mult already stacks matchup(log5 starter)+park+weather+lineup-exposure, and batter_hits blends xBA in two places; expected_runs' mu_R RE-ENCODES the opposing starter (via staff_suppression) and offense (via offense_factor). So a naive gamma on top of matchup_mult+lineup_mult would double-count the opposing-staff and slot signals. RESOLUTION (REPLACE, not stack): when the GameContext is complete and game_ctx_mult is active for batter_hits, the runtime REPLACES matchup_mult AND lineup_mult with {gamma * slot_share_i} — the team-level opposing-staff adjustment now lives in gamma (team-hit target already encodes opposing staff/offense) and the per-batter slot exposure lives in the allocation (n_i uses slot AB share). Park+weather STAY (the expected_runs xwOBA factors are neutral-park Savant aggregates, so no overlap). The xBA blend stays ONLY in phase-1 p_AB level (via _dist_p_over) and is NOT re-applied inside gamma, because S is built from the RAW recency hits/AB rate (not base_proj, not the xBA-blended mean) — mirroring how _distributional_over_rate deliberately rebuilds from raw hits/AB to avoid the §2.4a mean double-count. Offline parity: the join-time gamma is likewise computed from each obs's raw prior_games rate, and project_and_empirical applies gamma in place of (not on top of) any matchup term — matchup was never in the offline seam anyway, so offline only needs the REPLACE semantics enforced at runtime.

## phasing
Phase 0 (no behavior change, shippable): factor mlb_starters.build_team_run_pmf public (extract inner probabilities() from negative_binomial_margin_probability); add game_context.py with GameContext build (mu_R + run_pmf + convolved total + rho + lineup), completeness/TTL cache, leakage-safe as-of. Unit tests on PMF sums-to-1, convolution moments, leakage cutoff. Phase 1 (FIRST GATE VERDICT, independently shippable): per-game augmentation in join_book_lines_to_actuals attaching obs['game_ctx_mult']=gamma for batter_hits; ride the existing feat_mult scalar seam in project_and_empirical; run select_method_at_real_lines -> gamma either clears MIN_CALIB_BRIER_GAIN in both folds or ships nowhere (honest, like rest). Phase 2 (runtime, two-way parity): game_ctx_mult in props combined_mult REPLACING matchup+lineup for batter_hits when GameContext complete; cache per (home,away,date); parity test offline<->runtime; also wire mu_R into analyze_totals_value/_mlb_expected_runs_projection to unify the three run estimates (behind a flag, separately gated). Phase 3 (full-marginal gate verdict): project_game_joint p_dist + method "G" in _score_abc_real+candidate_methods+select loop; gate verdict on mean+variance. Phase 4 (correlation, most expensive): same-game parlay-CLV harness in backtest_props.py; requires the DK closing-line capture (per memory, decided but NOT YET BUILT) for the CLV leg.

## why_it_wins
It is the only design that delivers the roadmap's actual §3.1 vision — one shared run/total distribution that batter props sample from and reconcile bottom-up — instead of yet another independent per-batter scalar. It unifies the three currently-unreconciled run estimates (totals scalar, expected-runs spread joint, per-batter matchup) onto ONE leakage-safe source, and it resurrects the full run joint that mlb_starters already computes and then throws away. Crucially it confronts the validation problem head-on rather than pretending the single-prop Brier gate can see a joint model: it decomposes the model into three layers and gives each the RIGHT gate — the mean shift rides the existing, trusted real-line Brier gate via the existing scalar seam (so Phase 1 ships a real verdict cheaply and honestly), the full marginal gets a new method 'G' that the gate is partially sensitive to, and correlation — which no Brier can see — gets the only instrument that can measure it, a parlay-CLV harness. It reuses every existing stdlib primitive (PMF recurrence, hits_at_least, weighted moments, norm_ppf nodes) with no MC and no new deps, caches once per game, and fails open to today's pricing whenever the context is incomplete. It is maximally faithful and maximally correct while being brutally honest that the payoff may, like every prior candidate, clear the gate nowhere.

## top_risks
- rho (league hits-per-run) is a crude first-moment map; team hits<->runs is noisy, so gamma may inject noise and fail MIN_CALIB_BRIER_GAIN exactly like rest/xBA/CSW/NegBin-E did — the roadmap ships nothing that can't clear the gate, and this may clear nowhere.
- expected_runs completeness is often False (missing probable starter / Savant coverage gaps warned in get_expected_runs_team_factors), so GameContext.complete=False frequently -> feature fails open on many games -> thin gate n, the same 'too thin to confirm' fate as pitcher_outs (n=60).
- Synthetic-sweep parity is permanently broken for this feature (accepted, matchup_mult precedent) — the auto-adoption sweep axis cannot size it; only the manual real-line method gate can, reducing the automation the roadmap prizes.
- Correlation is the whole point of a JOINT model but is invisible to single-prop Brier; its only validator (parlay-CLV) depends on the DK closing-line capture that is decided-but-unbuilt, and on sparse realized same-game parlay outcomes — Phase 4 may be unvalidatable for a while.
- Double-count REPLACE semantics (gamma replacing matchup_mult+lineup_mult) is subtle; a wiring error silently double-counts opposing-staff and regresses live pricing — mitigated only by the two-way parity + REPLACE unit tests.
- Leakage trap: any accidental use of get_team_offense_splits/get_team_bullpen_quality/get_pitcher_quality (season-to-date) inside the validation GameContext invalidates the confirmation folds, which assume leakage is absent and cannot detect it.
- Perf: the offline per-game pre-pass adds an as-of Savant expected_runs fetch per game-date in the refit (cached 24h) and a lineup fetch; acceptable but adds refit wall-time and a new network dependency to the calibration path.
